# Spotify-Wrapped
Create your own personalized "Spotify Wrapped" experience anytime during the year. Visualize your top tracks, artists, genres, and share your unique music taste with others.

## Features 📋
1. **Spotify Authentication**: Log in securely via Spotify OAuth.
2. **Top Stats**: View your top tracks, artists, and genres for different time ranges.
3. **Wrapped Animation**: Experience an animated slideshow of your stats.
4. **Custom Date Range**: Filter your stats by a custom date range.
5. **Social Sharing**: Share your "Wrapped" experience with friends via social media links.
6. **Download as Image**: Save your wrapped summary as an image.
7. **Dark Mode**: Toggle between light and dark themes for a better experience.
